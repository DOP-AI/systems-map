# Ecosystem Topology

## Mission

The DOP-AI ecosystem is designed as a layered operational architecture focused on prevention, transparency, resilience, and public-interest operational intelligence.

Built under the foundational doctrine:

> Dignity Over Power

The ecosystem is intentionally modular.

Each repository represents a specialized operational layer while remaining interoperable with the larger system.

---

# Core Principle

```text id="ujlxnk"
Operational ecosystems become more resilient when relationships are visible and modular.
```
